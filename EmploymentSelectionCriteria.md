# Employment Selection Criteria

- Staff or senior level software engineer role
- Python as the main computer language, and can be combined with others (Rust, Go, TypeScript, etc.)
- Located in Munich, or fully remote in Germany (not open to relocation)
- Company with dependable WLB, good employee reviews, and stable business strategy (looking to commit long-term)
- Full-time, permanent, and direct employment contract
- No consultant/forward-deployed/solution-engineer type of arrangement
